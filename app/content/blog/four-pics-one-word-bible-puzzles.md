4 Pics 1 Word is the most playful game on Brethreign. Each puzzle is a row of four emoji that together hint at a Bible story, character, miracle or parable. You work out what they're pointing to and pick the answer from four choices. It's quick, it's fun, and children often beat the adults.

## Start a round

Open the Games page at [/games](/games) and tap **4 Pics 1 Word**. The start screen asks "What story is this?" Tap **▶ Start puzzles**.

## How a round works

A round is five puzzles.

1. **Look at the four emoji.** Read them together, not one at a time.
2. **Think of the story.** What happens in the Bible that brings those four things together?
3. **Pick the answer.** There are four choices and one is right.
4. **See the answer** with a short note, then tap **Next**.

## How to read the clues

Take the puzzle in the picture above: 🦁 🕳️ 🙏 👑. A lion, a pit, prayer and a crown. Put together, they point to Daniel in the lions' den: a man who prayed, was thrown to the lions by order of the king, and was kept safe.

Some ways in:

- **Find the strangest emoji.** A lion, a whale or a donkey narrows things down fast.
- **Look for a sequence.** Some puzzles tell the story in order, from beginning to end, left to right.
- **Think in types.** Is it a miracle, a parable, a person or a place? The choices often give it away.
- **Say it out loud.** Describing the four emoji in a sentence often brings the story to mind.

## Scoring

Each right answer is a point, so a round is out of five. Your score is added to the weekly leaderboard, and you can share your result to your feed, copy a share line or play again.

## A family favourite

Because the clues are pictures, 4 Pics 1 Word works for all ages. Younger children who can't read long questions can still play. A few ideas:

- **Play at the dinner table.** Show the phone and let everyone guess before anyone taps.
- **Tell the story after.** When someone gets it, ask them to tell the whole story in their own words.
- **Make your own.** Challenge your children to make a four-emoji puzzle for a story they love and test the rest of the family.

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> Sunday school teachers can use a round as a five-minute warm-up. Put the phone on a screen and let the class call out answers.</aside>

## Stories that stay

Jesus often taught with pictures: seeds, lamps, sheep and coins. A good picture helps a truth stay with us. The emoji in this game work in a small way like that, bringing the stories back to mind.

> "All these things spake Jesus unto the multitude in parables; and without a parable spake he not unto them." (Matthew 13:34)
