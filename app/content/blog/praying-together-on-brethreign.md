Some weeks you have the words and some weeks you don't. Brethreign was built so that nobody has to carry a burden alone. This guide explains how to ask for prayer, how to stand with others in theirs, and how to gather a small, private circle of people who pray together.

> Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ. (Galatians 6:2)

## Asking for prayer

You can post a prayer request from almost anywhere.

1. **Open the composer.** On the feed, tap **Prayer request** under "What breath do you have today?", or tap the box itself.
2. **Write what's on your heart.** Share as much or as little as you like. A sentence is enough.
3. **Tick 🙏 Prayer request.** This marks your breath as a request, so others know you're asking them to pray.
4. **Choose where it goes.** Pick the **Prayer Requests** room, or post it in any room or to the community feed.
5. **Decide whether to put your name to it.** Tick **Anonymous** if you'd rather not. Your request is still seen and prayed for.
6. **Tap Breathe.**

<aside class="bl-note"><b>Good to know.</b> Anonymous is there for the requests that feel too tender to sign: a marriage under strain, a struggle you haven't told anyone, a diagnosis you're still taking in. Use it whenever you need to.</aside>

## The Prayer Requests and Prayer Warriors rooms

Rooms are themed spaces anyone can join. Two are given over to prayer.

**Prayer Requests** is where members bring their needs. Its description puts it simply: "Submit your prayer requests and join others in prayer. Let's bear one another's burdens and lift each other up in prayer." Tap **Join Room** to follow along, or **Share Here** to post straight into it.

**Prayer Warriors** is another room centred on prayer, a good one to join if you want to spend time praying for others.

## Praying for someone else

When you read a prayer request, tap **🙏 Praying** to join in. The count on the request shows how many people are praying.

That number can mean a great deal to someone in a hard place. It says: you are not alone in this.

If you'd like to say more, leave a comment. A short word of encouragement, a verse that has helped you, or simply "Praying for you this morning" is often exactly enough. On a computer, the right-hand side of the feed also lists open prayer requests with a **Pray** button beside each one.

## The Wall of Answered Prayers

Prayers are answered, and those answers deserve to be remembered. When a prayer request is marked answered, it takes its place on the [Wall of Answered Prayers](/answered).

The Wall is a place to read slowly. Each testimony there was once someone's long night, carried by others in prayer until morning came. Visiting it is a good way to build your faith before you pray for the next request.

## Prayer Circles: close company

Some prayers belong to a small group: your family, your cell group, the friends you've prayed with for years. That's what [Prayer Circles](/circles) are for.

A circle is a small, private room. It has a quiet stream that only its members can see, and one shared prayer list that you carry together. There are no ads in circles.

To start one:

1. **Go to Prayer Circles.** Open [/circles](/circles), or find Prayer Circles in the menu.
2. **Name your circle.** For example, "Tuesday Cell Group" or "Family prayers".
3. **Add what gathers you (optional).** A line about why you pray together.
4. **Tap Gather the circle.**
5. **Share the invite link.** You'll get a private invite link. That link is the only way in, so send it only to the people you actually pray with.

## A word on care

Prayer requests are often personal. Please pray for what people share without passing their stories on. If someone asks for prayer about a situation involving others, keep details gentle and private, as you would in a church prayer meeting. And if anything you read worries you, you can report it in one tap so a moderator can look at it.
