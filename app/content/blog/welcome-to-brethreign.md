Brethreign is a free Christian social network: a calm, safe place online where believers can share what's on their heart, pray for one another, read the Bible together and stay close to the people they walk with. This page explains what it is, who it's for and what you can do once you join.

## A home for believers, wherever you are

Church fellowship doesn't stop when the service ends. Most of us keep it going in group chats, phone calls and the odd social media post squeezed between adverts and arguments. Brethreign gives that fellowship a home of its own.

It's for any Christian, anywhere. Members join from around the world, and the site works on any phone or computer. There's also an Android app on Google Play, in testing now and opening to everyone in January 2027.

Joining is free for every member. You sign up with a username, email and password, or with your Google account.

## What you can do here

### Breathe

A post on Brethreign is called a **breath**. Each day the feed greets you with the date and a simple question: "What breath do you have today?" You can share a thought, a testimony, a photo, a voice note or a Bible verse. Longer conversations can be started as threads.

### Pray

Prayer sits at the centre of Brethreign. You can post a prayer request in the Prayer Requests room or anywhere else, and you can post it anonymously if you'd rather not attach your name. Others tap "🙏 Praying" to stand with you. When God answers, the request can join the Wall of Answered Prayers. For your family or cell group there are Prayer Circles: small private groups with a shared prayer list.

### Read

Open the Bible from anywhere on Brethreign to browse, search, copy or share a passage. Every verse has its own page showing what members have breathed about it. Reading plans such as "30 Days in the Psalms", "John in 21 Days" and "Proverbs in 31 Days" give you one passage a day.

### Chat

Private one-to-one chats let you send text, photos, voice notes, documents and Bible verses. A scam guard warns about messages that ask for money, bank details or codes, and you can block anyone from messaging you.

### Play

The Games page has a Bible Quiz with a weekly leaderboard, a Chess Puzzle of the Day and a Chess Ladder, plus Character Match, Church History, Verse Memory and more.

### Grow professionally

New this month, every member can build a professional profile: a headline, experience, education, certifications, languages and skills, plus a portfolio of work you're proud of. You can even fill it in quickly by importing from LinkedIn.

## Rooms for every season of life

Rooms are themed spaces anyone can join. There are rooms for prayer and Bible study, such as Prayer Warriors, Scripture Reflections and Questions & Guidance. There are rooms for seasons of life: Young Adults, Teenagers, Single Brothers, Single Ladies, The Married Room, Parents Room and Seniors' Corner. And there are rooms for work and interests, including Brethren in Tech, Brethren in Business, Music & Worship, Book Club and Recipe Room. Browse them all at [/rooms](/rooms).

## What makes Brethreign different

**It's built to be safe.** AI checks breaths, comments, chats, photos and portfolio pieces for abuse, explicit content, occult content and scams. You can report anything in one tap, and our moderators, who are real people, review it. Our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines) set out what's welcome.

**Your privacy is yours to set.** You can make your profile private in Settings → Privacy. Prayer requests can be anonymous. Your Brethren Card, which holds personal details like your WhatsApp number, is only ever shown to people you've connected with.

**Some places stay quiet.** There are no ads in prayer, chats or Prayer Circles.

**It's free.** Every member gets everything described here at no cost.

**It's yours to make comfortable.** Choose a light or dark theme and your own accent colour. "Scripture rain", the verses drifting softly behind the page, can be switched off if you prefer a plainer screen.
