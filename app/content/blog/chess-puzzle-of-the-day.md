Every day Brethreign brings you a new chess puzzle from Lichess, the free and open chess site. You play it on a board right inside Brethreign, and solving it earns points for the weekly leaderboard. It takes a few minutes and is a good habit to keep. Here's how it works.

## Find today's puzzle

Open the Games page at [/games](/games) and tap **Chess Puzzle of the Day**, or go straight to [/games/chess](/games/chess). The daily puzzle sits at the top of the page.

## Read the top of the puzzle first

Before you move a piece, read the lines above the board:

- **Whose move it is.** For example, "Daily puzzle · White to move". You always play the side that's moving.
- **The rating.** How hard the puzzle is, using Lichess's rating scale.
- **The themes.** Clues such as "Fork", "Pin" or "Mate In2". If it says Mate In2, you're looking for checkmate in two moves.
- **The points.** A badge on the right shows how many points you'll earn if you solve it.

## How many points

Harder puzzles are worth more:

| Puzzle rating | Points if solved |
|---|---|
| Under 1500 | 1 |
| 1500 to 1999 | 2 |
| 2000 to 2499 | 3 |
| 2500 and above | 5 |

You can claim points from the daily puzzle **once a day**. After that you can keep playing, but the points are already in.

## Playing the puzzle

1. **Find the best move.** Drag or tap a piece to move it.
2. **The other side replies.** If your move is right, the board plays the opponent's answer and it's your turn again.
3. **Keep going until the line is finished.** When the last move is played, the puzzle is solved and your points are added. You'll see a message such as "+2 points added".

If a move isn't the best one, the board tells you and you can try again.

## Hints, Restart and Show solution

Three buttons sit under the board:

- **💡 Hint** points you in the right direction.
- **↺ Restart** puts the pieces back to the start of the puzzle.
- **▶ Show solution** plays the answer for you. You won't earn points for that puzzle once you've seen it, but you will see the idea, and that's how chess players improve.

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> Look for checks, captures and threats first, in that order. Most puzzle answers start with one of the three.</aside>

## After the daily puzzle

Solved it, or want more? Scroll down to the **Chess Ladder**, a climb of ten puzzles that get harder as you go. The ladder doesn't add points, so you can practise as much as you like. We explain it fully in [Climb the Chess Ladder](/blog/climb-the-chess-ladder).

## Why chess on a faith app

Chess teaches patience, thinking before acting, and looking at things from the other side of the board. Those are good habits for any of us. And a daily puzzle is a gentle reason to come back to Brethreign, where a prayer request or a friend's breath may be waiting too.
