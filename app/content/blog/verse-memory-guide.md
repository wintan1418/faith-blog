Verse Memory is a short game for learning scripture by heart. Each round gives you five different verses from the King James Version, each with a word missing, and asks you to fill the gap. It takes a couple of minutes, and if you play it often, the verses start to stay with you.

## Start a round

Open the Games page at [/games](/games) and tap **Verse Memory**. The start screen asks "How well do you know it?" Tap **▶ Start round**.

## How a round works

Every round has five questions, each on a different verse:

1. **Questions 1 to 4: fill the blank.** You see a verse quoted exactly as the KJV has it, with one word or phrase missing. Pick the missing words from four choices.
2. **Question 5: finish the verse.** The last question gives you the start of a verse and asks you to choose how it ends.

After each answer you'll see whether you were right, along with the reference, so you know where the verse lives in the Bible.

## Verses change every round

The verses come from rotating themed sets, so each round feels different. Themes include God's love, faith, courage and strength, the Psalms, the promises of God, the words of Jesus, wisdom from Proverbs, comfort in trials, prayer, new life in Christ, hope and heaven, and holiness and obedience.

You'll meet well-loved verses such as John 3:16, Joshua 1:9, Psalm 23:1, Proverbs 3:5-6 and Philippians 4:13, and some you may not know as well.

## Scoring

Each right answer is a point, so a round is out of five. Your score goes to the weekly leaderboard, and a perfect round counts toward the Perfect Round badge. At the end you can share your result to your feed, copy a share line or play again.

## How to actually remember verses

The game helps, but memory grows with a little extra effort. A few habits that work:

- **Say the verse out loud** after the round, including the reference.
- **Pick one verse a day** from your round and write it somewhere you'll see it.
- **Read the verses around it.** Open the reference in the Bible on Brethreign. A verse in its setting is easier to remember and harder to misuse.
- **Come back tomorrow.** Short, regular practice beats one long session.
- **Breathe on it.** Share a verse that spoke to you as a breath. Explaining why it matters fixes it in your mind.

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> Play Verse Memory with your children at bedtime. Read the verse aloud with the gap and let them guess before you show the choices.</aside>

## Why memorise scripture

A verse you know by heart is with you when you have no phone and no Bible to hand: in a hospital waiting room, in a hard conversation, in the middle of the night. It's also ready when a friend needs encouragement.

> "Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee." (Psalm 119:11)
