Brethreign was built around scripture, so the Bible is never more than a tap away. This guide shows you how to open it from anywhere, what you will find on each verse's page, how reading plans work, and the easiest ways to share a verse with someone who needs it.

> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path. (Psalm 119:105)

## Open the Bible from anywhere

You do not need to go hunting for a separate Bible app. **Open Bible** is available from anywhere on Brethreign, so you can look up a passage while you are reading the feed, writing a breath or chatting with a friend.

Inside the panel you can:

1. **Browse.** Pick a book from the Old Testament or New Testament list and read.
2. **Search.** Switch to the Search tab and type a word or phrase when you remember part of a verse but not where it is.
3. **Copy.** Copy a verse to paste wherever you need it.
4. **Share.** Send a verse on to someone, inside Brethreign or beyond it.

## Every verse has its own page

Each verse on Brethreign has a page of its own. At the top is the scripture itself. Below it, under "Breaths that carry this verse", you will see what members have breathed about it: reflections, testimonies and longer threads.

This is one of the quieter joys of Brethreign. Open Psalm 23 and you can read how other believers have leaned on it in hard seasons and good ones.

### Breathe on this verse

If a verse speaks to you, tap **Breathe on this verse** and write what God showed you through it. The breaths on a verse's page come from members doing exactly that, so the next person who opens the verse may be encouraged by what you wrote.

## Reading plans

Reading a whole book of the Bible is easier when someone hands you one passage a day. That is what reading plans are for. You will find them at [/reading_plans](/reading_plans).

The plans available are:

- **30 Days in the Psalms.** One Psalm a day, for a calm rhythm.
- **John in 21 Days.** One chapter a day through John's gospel.
- **Proverbs in 31 Days.** One chapter for every day of the month.

<figure class="bl-shot">
  <img src="/blog/shots/reading-plans.jpg" alt="The Reading plans page, titled 'Slow rhythms in scripture', showing three plans with Start this plan and Preview buttons: 30 Days in the Psalms, John in 21 Days and Proverbs in 31 Days">
  <figcaption>Pick a plan and get one passage a day.</figcaption>
</figure>

To begin:

1. **Open Reading plans.** Go to [/reading_plans](/reading_plans).
2. **Have a look first.** Tap **Preview** to see what the plan covers.
3. **Start.** Tap **Start this plan** when you are ready.
4. **Read and check in.** Each day, read that day's passage and check in to keep your rhythm.

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> If you are new to daily reading, Proverbs in 31 Days is a gentle start. The chapters are short and full of practical wisdom.</aside>

## Share a verse

Scripture is meant to be passed on. There are a few easy ways to do it.

### In a breath

On the feed, tap the **Share a verse** chip under "What breath do you have today?", or add a Bible verse from inside the composer when you take a breath. You can also use **Suggest scripture** in the composer if you have written something and would like a verse to go with it.

### In a chat

In any private chat, tap the book icon under the message box, pick a verse and send it. A verse in a private message can be exactly the word someone needed that day. Read more in our [guide to private chats](/blog/private-chats-guide).

### Outside Brethreign

From the Open Bible panel you can copy or share a verse to send it wherever your friends and family are.

## Verse of the day

Brethreign also offers a verse of the day: one verse to carry with you. It is a simple way to start the day in the Word, even on mornings when there is no time for a full reading.

<aside class="bl-note"><b>Good to know.</b> The drifting verses you may see in the background of Brethreign are called "scripture rain". If you prefer a plain background, you can switch it off.</aside>
