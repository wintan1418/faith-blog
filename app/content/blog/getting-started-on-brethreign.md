You've found Brethreign and you'd like to settle in. This guide walks you through your first ten minutes: creating your account, setting up your profile, finding people and rooms, and sharing your first post. By the end, Brethreign will feel like yours.

## 1. Create your account

<figure class="bl-shot">
  <img src="/blog/shots/signup.jpg" alt="The Join Brethreign sign-up form with Username, Email and Password fields and a Create account button">
  <figcaption>Three fields and you're in. You can also join with Google.</figcaption>
</figure>

1. **Open the sign-up page.** Go to [brethreign.com/users/sign_up](/users/sign_up) on your phone or computer.
2. **Choose a username.** Letters, numbers and underscores. This is how people find you, so pick something you're happy for your church friends to see.
3. **Add your email and a password.** Use at least 8 characters.
4. **Tap Create account.** If you'd rather not make a new password, sign up with your Google account instead.

Joining is free, and every feature in this guide is free too.

## 2. Add a photo and a short bio

A face and a few words help people recognise you and feel at ease saying hello.

1. **Open your profile.** Tap your photo or go to Profile, then tap **Edit Profile**.
2. **Add a profile photo.** A clear photo of your face works best.
3. **Write a short bio.** A line or two is plenty: what you do, where you worship, or a sentence about your walk with God.
4. **Fill in your city and faith background if you like.** These show on your profile and help people with something in common find you.

## 3. Complete your Brethren Card

Your Brethren Card is a private card for the people you connect with. It holds your church or assembly, what you do, a short "about you", and, if you choose, your WhatsApp number and email. Only your connections ever see it.

You need a complete card before you can send connection requests, so it's worth doing now.

1. **Open Settings → Brethren Card.** Or go straight to [/settings/brethren_card/edit](/settings/brethren_card/edit).
2. **Fill in Church / Assembly, What You Do and About You.**
3. **Add contact details only if you're comfortable.** Your WhatsApp number and email are optional. Share only what you'd happily give a new friend from church.
4. **Save.** You'll see a green **Complete** badge.

## 4. Follow a few people and join some rooms

Your feed comes alive once you follow people and join rooms.

1. **Visit the People page.** Search by username, bio, location or faith background. Each person has **Follow** and **Connect** buttons.
2. **Look at "Brethren you may know".** These suggestions come from your church, your connections, your work, your city and your rooms. Tap **See all** for the full list, or tap × to dismiss someone.
3. **Follow a handful of people.** Following means you'll see their breaths in your Following tab.
4. **Join two or three rooms.** Go to [/rooms](/rooms) and pick a few that fit your life: Prayer Requests, Bible Study, Young Adults, Parents Room, Brethren in Tech, Book Club and many more. Tap **Join Room**.

<figure class="bl-shot">
  <img src="/blog/shots/people.jpg" alt="The People page headed 'Find people walking the same road', with a search box and a 'Brethren you may know' row showing two suggestion cards with + Connect buttons">

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> Following and connecting are different. Following lets you see someone's breaths. Connecting, through "Can I Know You More?", shares Brethren Cards once you both agree.</aside>

## 5. Take your first breath

1. **Go to the feed.** At the top you'll see today's date and the question "What breath do you have today?"
2. **Tap the box.** The composer opens.
3. **Write a few words.** A hello, a verse that's carried you this week, or something you're thankful for. You can add a photo, a voice note or a Bible verse too.
4. **Tap Breathe.** That's it. You've also earned your First Breath badge.

Come back and breathe again tomorrow to start a streak: the number of days in a row you've shared.

## 6. Make it comfortable

A few small choices make Brethreign feel like home.

1. **Pick a theme.** Light or dark, whichever is easier on your eyes.
2. **Choose an accent colour.** Settings has an Accent tab for your own colour.
3. **Decide about scripture rain.** These are the verses that drift softly behind the page. Many members love them. If you'd prefer a plainer screen, you can switch them off.
4. **Check your privacy.** In [Settings → Privacy](/settings/privacy/edit), "Make my profile public" is where you decide who sees your posts.
