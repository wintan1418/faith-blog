Your professional profile tells brethren what you do and where you serve. This guide walks through every field in Settings → Professional, with examples for different kinds of work, so you can finish a good profile in one sitting.

<aside class="bl-tip"><b>Tip.</b> Already have a LinkedIn profile? Start with [Import your LinkedIn profile](/blog/import-your-linkedin-profile), then come back here to check and polish what came across.</aside>

## Open Settings → Professional

1. **Go to your settings.** Open [Settings → Professional](/settings/professional/edit). It sits between the Profile and Portfolio tabs in Settings.
2. **Find "Headline & skills".** This first form holds your headline, industry, church and skills. Experience, education, certifications and languages come further down the page.

## Write your headline

Your headline is the one line under your name. It's the first thing people read on your profile, so make it clear before you make it clever. A good pattern is **what you do + where, or for whom**.

- Registered nurse at a teaching hospital
- Primary school teacher, Year 4
- Site engineer building affordable homes
- Accountant helping small businesses with tax and payroll
- Event planner for weddings and church conferences
- Fashion designer and tailor, made-to-measure
- Final-year student, Computer Science
- Pastor and Bible teacher

If you leave the headline blank, Brethreign uses your current role instead. That's fine as a start, but a headline you write yourself can say more.

<aside class="bl-note"><b>Good to know.</b> Keep your headline about your work. Your bio and Brethren Card are better places for your testimony and personal details.</aside>

## Choose your industry

Pick the industry closest to your work from the drop-down. The list is close to LinkedIn's, with options such as Software Development, Healthcare & Medicine, Education and Banking. If you work across two, choose the one you'd most like people to find you under.

## Pick your church or assembly

Choose your church from the list if it's there, rather than typing it. When you and others from your assembly pick the same entry, you're easier to find: your church is one of the things "Brethren you may know" uses to suggest people. Your church also shows on your profile.

## Add your skills

Type your skills separated by commas, up to 30. Mix the specific and the broad:

- **Nurse:** Patient care, Wound care, Triage, Health education, Record keeping
- **Teacher:** Lesson planning, Classroom management, Mathematics, Mentoring
- **Builder:** Bricklaying, Site supervision, Reading drawings, Costing
- **Software developer:** Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL, Payments, Testing

Add at least five. Five skills is one of the steps on your profile strength card, and shared skills help "Brethren you may know" suggest people in your field.

Save your changes before moving on.

## Add your experience

1. **Tap "+ Add" under Experience.** You can do this in Settings → Professional or straight from your Professional tab.
2. **Enter your title.** For example Software engineer, Nurse or Accountant.
3. **Enter the organisation.** The company, hospital, school, business or church.
4. **Choose the employment type.** Full-time is selected by default. The other options are Part-time, Self-employed, Freelance, Contract, Internship, Apprenticeship, Volunteer and "Church or ministry service".
5. **Add the location.** For example Lagos, Nigeria.
6. **Set the dates.** Tick "I currently work here" for your present role, then pick the start month. For a past role, add the end month too.
7. **Describe what you do.** This is optional, but two or three plain sentences help: what you're responsible for and one thing you're proud of.
8. **Save.** The role appears on your Professional tab.

### Church and volunteer roles

If you lead a youth group, sing in the choir, serve as an usher or volunteer with a charity, add it. Choose "Volunteer" or "Church or ministry service" as the employment type and it shows in a separate **Volunteering** section on your profile, while paid work stays under **Experience**. More on this in [Volunteering and church roles](/blog/volunteering-and-church-roles).

## Add your education

Under Education, tap "+ Add" and fill in:

- **School:** university, college, polytechnic, school or training institute
- **Qualification:** for example BSc, HND, Diploma or a professional certificate
- **Field of study:** for example Nursing or Civil Engineering
- **Years:** when you started and finished

Add each qualification that helps tell your story.

## Check how it looks

Open your profile and tap the **Professional** tab. You'll see your experience, volunteering, education and skills, plus the profile strength card at the top, which only you can see.
