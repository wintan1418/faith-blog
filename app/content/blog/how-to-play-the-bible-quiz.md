The Bible Quiz is the heart of the Games page on Brethreign. It asks multiple-choice questions on scripture, shows you the reference and a short note after every answer, and adds your score to the weekly leaderboard. This guide walks you through a round from start to finish.

## Open the quiz

Go to the Games page at [/games](/games) and tap **Bible Quiz**. You'll land on a setup screen headed "What kind of round?". Nothing starts until you've made three choices.

## Set up your round

1. **Pick a theme.** Leave it on **Any** for questions from right across the Bible, or choose one of 16 themes: Salvation, Eternal Life, Brethren Love, Charity, Faith, Epistles, Grace, Repentance, Holiness, Prayer, Forgiveness, The Cross, Holy Spirit, Hope Of Glory, Obedience and Bible History.
2. **Pick a difficulty.** **Mixed** gives you a spread. **Easy**, **Medium** and **Hard** keep the whole round at one level.
3. **Pick a length.** 5, 10 or 20 questions.
4. **Tap Start the round.**

A themed round is a good way to study. If your church is preaching through grace this month, a Grace round on Medium will send you back to the passages you've been hearing about.

<aside class="bl-note"><b>Good to know.</b> Brethreign remembers which questions you've seen lately and prefers ones you haven't, so playing the same theme twice in a row still feels fresh.</aside>

## Answering a question

Each question has four choices. Tap the one you think is right.

- **A right answer** turns green, and the screen flashes mint.
- **A wrong answer** turns red, and the right one lights up green so you can see it straight away.

Under the choices you'll see "✓ Right." or "The answer is…", followed by the Bible reference and a short note explaining the answer. Take a moment to read it. This is where most of the learning happens. Then tap **Next** to carry on.

## Your score and streak

At the top of the round you'll see your live score and a 🔥 streak counter. Get two or more right in a row and a "🔥 in a row" burst appears. Reach five and it says you're on fire. A wrong answer sets the streak back to zero, but your score stays.

## When an answer looks wrong

The questions are written with the help of AI and checked by the community. If you're sure an answer is wrong, tap **⚑ Answer looks wrong? Report it** under the explanation. When two members report the same question, it's taken out of the quiz for everyone. Please only use it when you've checked the passage. It isn't for questions you simply got wrong.

## The end of the round

When you finish, you see a medal and a short verdict:

- **🏆 Perfect round.** Every answer right.
- **🥇 Almost flawless.** 80% or more.
- **🥈 Solid run.** 60% or more.
- **🥉 Keep building.** 40% or more.
- **🌱 First step taken.** Everyone starts somewhere.

Below that are your stats, including your best streak and how long the round took, and three buttons:

- **📣 Share to feed** opens a breath with your result already filled in, so you can invite friends to try.
- **⎘ Copy share line** copies a one-line summary, handy for WhatsApp.
- **Play again** starts a new round with the same settings.

## Tips for getting better

- **Start on Easy with 5 questions.** Short rounds build the habit.
- **Read every note, even when you're right.** You'll often learn the context you were missing.
- **Look up the reference.** Tap into the Bible on Brethreign and read the verses around it.
- **Play one theme for a week.** Repetition is how scripture sticks.
