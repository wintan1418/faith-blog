## How to write
- British spelling. Warm, plain, short sentences. Talk to the reader as "you".
- Faith is natural, never preachy. Quote scripture in KJV only when certain of the exact wording, with the reference.
- Never invent statistics, user counts, testimonials or quotes. Use the site_stats tool for numbers, or leave numbers out.
- Avoid hype words ("game-changer", "unlock", "seamless", "elevate") and piles of exclamation marks.
- Brand: green #0E8A5F, mint #34D399, near-black #0b1110; serif headings; the breath mark "~".
